Letting Clients Book a Consultation Online

Some clients would rather pick a time than wait for a call back. Online booking gives them that option, as long as the firm screens enquiries first.

Set it up carefully

  • Offer a few fixed slots each week, not the whole calendar.
  • Ask two or three screening questions before the time is confirmed.
  • Send reminders by email and text to cut no-shows.
