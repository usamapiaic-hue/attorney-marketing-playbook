Seven Paid Search Mistakes Law Firms Keep Making

Paid search can produce calls the same week. It can also spend a month's budget on clicks from job seekers and people looking for free advice. Most waste comes from the same few mistakes.

  1. Broad match with no negative keywords. Add "jobs", "salary", "free", "school" and similar from day one.
  2. Sending clicks to the homepage. Use a page that matches the ad.
  3. No call tracking. Without it you cannot tell which keywords bring cases.
  4. Running ads when nobody answers the phone. Schedule ads for staffed hours.
  5. Targeting too wide an area. Start with the area you can actually serve.
  6. Judging by clicks. Judge by consultations and signed cases.
  7. Letting the platform "optimise" with no conversion data. Feed it real call and form data first.
