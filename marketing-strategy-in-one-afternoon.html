A Law Firm Marketing Strategy in One Afternoon

Many firms spend on marketing before agreeing what it is for. One afternoon with the partners can fix that.

  1. Write down the three matter types the firm wants more of.
  2. For each, describe the typical client and where they look for help.
  3. Pick one or two channels that reach them.
  4. Set a monthly budget and a date to review results.
