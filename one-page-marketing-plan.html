A One-Page Marketing Plan for a Small Firm

Long marketing plans tend to stay in a drawer. A single page, reviewed every quarter, gets used. Fill in each box in a sentence or two.

Who we help: the clients and cases we want more of.
Where they look: search, referrals, directories, social, community.
What we say: one clear reason to choose us, in plain words.
Channels this year: no more than three, done well.
Budget: a monthly figure and how it splits across the channels.
Measure: calls, consultations and signed cases per channel.
Review: the date each quarter when we check and adjust.
