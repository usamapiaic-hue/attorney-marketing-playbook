Law Firm Content That Actually Gets Calls

Many law firm blogs publish news summaries and general legal trivia that bring visitors who never call. Content that brings enquiries answers the questions people ask just before they hire someone.

Pick topics close to hiring

  • "How much does a [case type] lawyer cost in [city]?"
  • "What happens at the first meeting?"
  • "How long does [process] take in [state]?"
  • "Do I need a lawyer for [situation]?"

Write for the reader, then the search engine

Answer the question in the first paragraph. Explain the details. End with a clear next step and the phone number. Link to the related practice page.

Fewer, better pieces

One thorough article a month that an attorney has reviewed beats four thin ones.
