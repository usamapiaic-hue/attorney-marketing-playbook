Write Content Only Your State's Lawyers Could Write

Generic legal content is everywhere. Pages that mention the state's courts, deadlines and terminology are rarer and more useful, and they signal real local knowledge.

Play: for each practice page, add one paragraph that only a lawyer in your state could write: a local court, a state deadline, or how a process differs from neighbouring states.

Examples from Legal Web Agency: law firm website content for Pennsylvania and Texas.
