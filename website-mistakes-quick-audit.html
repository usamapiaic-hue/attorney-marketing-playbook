A 15-Minute Law Firm Website Self-Check

Before paying for a full audit, a partner or office manager can spot the most common website problems in fifteen minutes with a phone and a notepad.

On your phone

  • Can you tap the phone number on the homepage without scrolling?
  • Does every practice page say which city or state you serve?
  • Does the contact form work? Send yourself a test.

On a computer

  • Are attorney bios current, with real photos?
  • Is the copyright year and office address up to date?
  • Do old blog posts link to pages that no longer exist?
  • Is the required advertising disclaimer present?

In search

Search your firm name and your main service plus city. Note what appears, and whether the Google Business Profile shows the right phone and hours.
