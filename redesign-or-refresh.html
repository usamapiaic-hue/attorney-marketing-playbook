Redesign or Refresh? A Quick Test

Not every tired website needs a full rebuild. Some need better words and photos, not new code.

Refresh if the site works well on phones, loads reasonably and is easy to edit, but the content and photos are dated.
Redesign if it is hard to use on a phone, hard to update, or built on a platform nobody in the office can manage.
