How Small Law Firms Earn Links Without Buying Them

Links from other websites still help a law firm's pages rank. Buying them is risky and often against search engine rules. Earning them is slower but lasts.

Plays that work for small firms

  • Local organisations: chambers of commerce, bar associations and charities you already support often list members or sponsors.
  • Useful local resources: a guide to your county's court process or a list of local support services gets cited by others.
  • Expert comments: reporters need quotes on legal questions; respond quickly and plainly.
  • Talks and teaching: CLE sessions, community talks and podcasts usually link to the speaker.
  • Directories that matter: a handful of real legal directories, kept consistent.

The Legal Web Agency guide to link building for lawyers covers each in more detail.
