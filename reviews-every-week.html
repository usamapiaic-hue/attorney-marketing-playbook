A Weekly Habit for Client Reviews

Most firms ask for reviews in bursts and then forget for months. A small weekly habit works better and looks more natural.

The routine

  1. Every Friday, list matters closed that week with a happy client.
  2. Send each one a short, personal message with a direct review link.
  3. Reply to every new review, good or bad, within a few days.

Stay within the rules

Never offer anything in return for a review, and check your state's rules before quoting reviews on the website.
