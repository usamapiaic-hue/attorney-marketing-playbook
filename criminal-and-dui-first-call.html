Criminal Defense and DUI: Winning the First Call

Someone arrested last night does not compare ten firms over a week. They call the first lawyer who answers and sounds capable. For criminal defense and DUI firms, marketing is mostly about being reachable at the right moment.

The play

1. Answer calls around the clock, through an answering service if needed.
2. Put "available 24/7" on the site only if it is true.
3. Show the courts you appear in, by name.
4. Explain the next steps after an arrest: arraignment, bail, license hearing for DUI.

DUI has a deadline clients miss

In many states a driver has only days to request a license hearing. A short, clear section on that deadline is both useful and a strong reason to call.
