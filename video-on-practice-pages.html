Put Video Where Clients Decide

Many firms record good videos and then post them only on social media, where they disappear within days. The same videos do more work on the website, next to the questions they answer.

1. Add a short attorney introduction to each attorney bio.
2. Put one FAQ answer video on each main practice page.
3. Add a "what happens at the first meeting" video to the contact page.
4. Caption everything and add a short text summary under each video.
